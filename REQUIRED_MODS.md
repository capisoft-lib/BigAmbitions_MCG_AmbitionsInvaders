# Required separate mods

- [LIB BA More Computer Games (MCG) 1.0.1+ on Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3793604724) (`LIB_BaComputerGames`), installed and enabled separately.

**Ambitions Invaders does not work without MCG installed and enabled.** Subscribe to the Workshop item above, wait for the download, then enable both mods in Big Ambitions. MCG is the only required mod library. FlappyAmbition is not required. Do not embed MCG's DLL.

For Steam publication, add [LIB MCG — Workshop item 3793604724](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3793604724) to Ambitions Invaders' **Required Items**. This document and the SDK manifest do not configure Steam automatically. Required Items link Workshop items without enforcing a semantic version; keep the MCG 1.0.1+ minimum in the description.
