[b][MCG] Ambitions Invaders 1.0.0[/b]

Your cash. Their faces. No negotiations.

Ambitions Invaders adds a horizontal arcade shooter to the computers in Big Ambitions through More Computer Games (MCG). Pilot a banknote in two dimensions and fire yellow lasers to the right. Your rivals are coming for your capital. This time, you can shoot back.

[b]A hostile takeover, with lasers[/b]
[list]
[*]Four familiar rivals in pixel art: Huang Guo, Ingrid Schneider, Jessica Johnson and Thierry Laurent Moreau.
[*]Progressively harder waves, enemy shots from wave two and a boss every four waves.
[*]Three shield points, with brief invulnerability after taking damage.
[*]Collisions, enemy shots and rivals escaping past you cost a shield point. No free passage for the competition.
[*]Completed rounds can improve your local personal best, managed by MCG.
[*]All 22 game languages, following the language selected in Big Ambitions.
[*]A dedicated promotional icon matching the Flappy Ambitions and Snacke series.
[/list]

[b]Requirements[/b]
[list]
[*]Big Ambitions 1.0 on Windows (Mono); build target: Build 3670 / Unity 2022.3.62f2.
[*][url=https://github.com/capisoft-lib/BigAmbitions_LIB_BA_MoreComputerGames][b]LIB BA More Computer Games (MCG) 1.0.0+[/b][/url], installed and enabled separately.
[/list]
This mod contains Ambitions Invaders only. MCG is the only required mod library. No MCG or game DLL is bundled. Flappy Ambitions and Snacke are not required. The original brick-breaker remains available through MCG.

[b]How to play[/b]
At a usable computer, choose [b]Play video games[/b], then [b]Ambitions Invaders[/b] in MCG.
[list]
[*][b]Arrow keys / WASD / ZQSD:[/b] move in two dimensions.
[*][b]Hold Space / left click:[/b] fire to the right.
[*][b]Space / left click / Enter:[/b] start or retry. R also retries.
[*][b]Tab:[/b] leave the computer through MCG.
[*][b]Backspace:[/b] return to MCG's game menu.
[*][b]Escape:[/b] open Big Ambitions' native pause menu.
[/list]
The round ends when your shield is exhausted. This version has no soundtrack.

[b]Records and upgrades[/b]
Only completed rounds count. Leaving during a round does not submit its score. Records remain local and are separated by profile, game and ruleset. Ambitions Invaders does not send network requests or directly modify native game saves.

The content folder is [b]MCG_AmbitionsInvaders[/b]. Keep only one active copy: back up and remove the old [b]AmbitionsInvaders[/b] folder from ModsLocal before upgrading. The technical IDs and record ruleset are preserved. Keep MCG's profile data.
